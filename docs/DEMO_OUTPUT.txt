
DIMENGUARD 1.0.0 | Compile-time units, clear formulas

  100 m / 10 s         = 10 m/s
  (100 m / 10 s) / 5 s = 2 m/s^2
  2 kg * acceleration  = 4 N
  1 m + cast<m>(1 ft)  = 1.3048 m
  72 km/h in m/s       = 20 m/s
  Area of 3 m x 4 m    = 12 m^2

  sizeof(quantity<metre>) = 8 bytes
  sizeof(double)          = 8 bytes

DIMENSION LAB | exponent order: Length, Mass, Time

distance / time -> velocity
  Left:   L^1 M^0 T^0
  Right:  L^0 M^0 T^1
  Status: VALID
  Result: L^1 M^0 T^-1
  Division subtracts the right-hand L, M, T exponents from the left-hand ones.

velocity / time -> acceleration
  Left:   L^1 M^0 T^-1
  Right:  L^0 M^0 T^1
  Status: VALID
  Result: L^1 M^0 T^-2
  Division subtracts the right-hand L, M, T exponents from the left-hand ones.

mass * acceleration -> force
  Left:   L^0 M^1 T^0
  Right:  L^1 M^0 T^-2
  Status: VALID
  Result: L^1 M^1 T^-2
  Multiplication adds the L, M, T exponents component by component.

1 metre + 1 second
  Left:   L^1 M^0 T^0
  Right:  L^0 M^0 T^1
  Status: REJECTED
  Addition/subtraction requires identical dimensions; these exponent vectors differ.
  Fix: Check the formula or the declared unit. unit_cast cannot convert length to time.

1 metre + 1 foot (before explicit conversion)
  Left:   L^1 M^0 T^0
  Right:  L^1 M^0 T^0
  Status: REJECTED
  Result: L^1 M^0 T^0
  Dimensions match, but unit scales differ. This library requires explicit conversion.
  Fix: Convert one operand with unit_cast to the other operand's unit, then add/subtract.
